#### 🏥 Medicare Claims Analysis with SQL: Signature Project
*Database Design & Knowledge Management*

Wrote and presented 10 SQL queries on the **CMS Synthetic Medicare Claims dataset** (beneficiary, inpatient, outpatient, and prescription drug tables). I framed each query as a real business question for a health plan or provider.
- Chronic disease combinations (diabetes, CHF, ischemic heart disease, COPD) and how they relate to cost
- Diabetes prevalence (29% of beneficiaries) and other chronic conditions
- High-cost prescriptions and inpatient claims; length of stay patterns
- Seasonal outpatient trends (January peak) and provider-level comparisons
- ICD-9 diagnosis filtering, Medicare Part A/B coverage, and outpatient-to-inpatient transitions
- Also built queries for a physical therapy clinic database using SQL and Query by Example (CPT codes, billing, therapist workload)

**Skills:** SQL · Microsoft Access · JOINs & Subqueries · Claims Data · ICD-9 / CPT

#### 📉 Drug Overdose Deaths in the U.S., 1999–2023: A 25-Year Analysis
*Introduction to Health Care Information Management*

Analyzed national surveillance data from **NIDA and CDC WONDER** in Excel to study trends and demographic patterns in overdose mortality. I also wrote a research paper and gave a presentation on the findings.
- Documented a **six-fold increase** in annual deaths (16,849 in 1999 to 105,000+ in 2023)
- Identified three epidemic waves: prescription opioids, heroin, and synthetic opioids
- Found the highest burden in adults aged **35–44** (crude rate 63.1 per 100,000) and a death rate about **2.4× higher for males**
- Calculated year-over-year change and percent change, and built trend and age-group charts

**Skills:** Excel · Public Health Data · Trend Analysis · Data Visualization · Research Writing

#### 📐 Biostatistics for Healthcare Data (Excel)
*MS coursework*

Applied statistical methods to hospital datasets (age, length of stay, DRG, charges, Medicare status):
- Descriptive statistics, frequency distributions, histograms, and scatter plots
- PivotTables and data cleaning (date correction, calculated length of stay)
- Random sampling methods (uniform, normal, RAND)
- 95% confidence intervals and t-tests
- **One-way ANOVA:** found a statistically significant difference in costs across four hospitals (F = 3.73, p = 0.013)
- **Regression with a dummy variable:** found no significant difference in cost by sex (p = 0.89)

**Skills:** Biostatistics · Hypothesis Testing · ANOVA · Regression · Excel Statistical Functions
